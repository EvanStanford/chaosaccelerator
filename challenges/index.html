Trick Shot

Each trick shot is a scene where, at one very particular starting point, the ball bounces off the other objects in a set order. On the map that order shows up as a region painted GOAL!. Zoom in until you find it, then click it. The Inspect card is your friend: hover the map to watch any starting point play out.

Analysis Maxing

Each of these hands you a target shape for the map's Rose Plot, the chart in the Analysis card that shows which way the sharp lines in a view run. Build any scene, open its map, and shape the view until every slice of the plot fits inside the target. Your closest attempt is kept, with a link back to that view.
